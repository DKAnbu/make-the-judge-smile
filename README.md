## The line

> This morning I found a warm croissant on my doorstep with a note: 'extra one, enjoy' — no name, just kindness with crumbs.

## Why it fits the judge's criteria

1. **A small good thing today** — it opens "This morning" and tells one small moment from today, nothing grand.
2. **Warmth** — the moment is an anonymous kindness: no name, just someone leaving a warm croissant on the doorstep.
3. **Surprise** — the line bends the unexpected way: a warm croissant and a mysterious note appear at the door, with no one to thank.
4. **Concrete** — small real details anchor it: the warm croissant, the note, the crumbs.
5. **Fresh wording** — "kindness with crumbs" is a new image, not a greeting-card phrase.

At 122 characters it is also short on purpose: the tie-break rewards the smaller text.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline), after validating the line is 1–200 characters, one line, free of control characters, with no leading or trailing whitespace, and ending in a single full stop.
